H's Attention panel is its desk terminal drawn with the same 1 November plan (in H a sheet covers it while planning).

H-HF draws only the first month, 14 November and 1 December, so its desk parts show 14 November (its Commit part is the paused month clock), its menu rack comes from 1 December, and it has no action menu, plan sheet or reputation dial. Its Attention panel is its desk terminal with the 1 November plan, as for H.
