01

The preferred direction: H

The game is about heading towards a disaster you may not be able to stop. These mock-ups give its main screen a look to match: a dark, worn office, with paper as the only light thing on screen. The layout keeps what already works. Every instrument (P(Doom), reputation, Attention) uses one flat, quiet meter style, which keeps the doom bands' colours. As doom rises, the room behind the controls darkens, but the controls stay where they are.

H is the preferred direction. The desk faces the player. Menus open from a rack of plates on the right. The month's plan sits on the desk, and Commit the Month is a small button with the hours held for events beside it.

H at the start of a new game: July 2017, nothing queued, P(Doom) at 20.0%. Click any screen to see it full size.

02

The date, typed in

A typewritten date replaces the calendar. It types itself in when a new month starts, with the heavy, punchy key strikes of a spy-film title card. The sound is made in the browser as it plays: there are no recorded samples. Press the button to hear it. Turn your sound on first.

NOVEMBER 1, 2017

The spec describes the sound so it can be made as a game audio asset. If you have reduced motion turned on, the date appears at once, but the sound still plays when you press the button.

03

Six moments of one run

Every design shows the same company at the same six moments. The run starts in month 1. In November 2017 the player chooses an action, plans the month, answers an event while the month plays out, and reads the month review. The last screen is a late month at high P(Doom). Names, costs and rules come from the game's data.

04

Three alternatives, same look

The three other layouts from the same study, rebuilt in H's darker look. Each keeps its own layout. Only the texture changed: the palette, meters, paper, the date and a smaller Commit button.

05

The spec for H

The spec covers the design's tokens, its components and their states, the six screens and their keys, how H maps onto today's Godot scene, the art and sound it needs, and the open rule questions. The repo copy is fork/design/hifi3/spec.md.
