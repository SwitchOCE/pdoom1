GALLERY: A C E G

All four Round 2 concepts belong in the gallery. E and A are among the better shots in their groups, C is upper-average, and G is average. Each one is better on this test than its Round 1 version, or than today's game for G. I judged them against the reference shots at full size, as a player would see a store screenshot: flavour, clarity, polish.

## A. The Desk
References:
- **`diegetic/shots/not-for-broadcast-1.jpg`:** hardware readouts framing one working screen. A's brass P(Doom) dial, needle meter and amber CRT are at that level of craft.
- **`diegetic/shots/papers-please-1.jpg`:** the desk is the screen. A's slips and folders (actions, door) are the same register, with clearer costs.
- **`diegetic/shots/frostpunk-1.jpg`:** one dominant instrument over a large view of the world. A's home now gives most of the frame to the office, as Frostpunk does.
- **`reference-games/shots/suzerain-1.jpg`:** turn-based planning from a desk.

**Verdict:** better. **Belongs here:** yes.

What holds it back:
1. The Actions menu is a large flat grid of 17 cards over a dimmed room. It reads more like a dashboard than the home does.
2. The bottom of the home screen still has three separate housings (terminal, button rack, Commit) competing for attention.

**Against Round 1:** better. The office and operator are now the picture; in Round 1 the room was a thin strip behind panels.

## C. The Paperwork Desk
References:
- **`diegetic/shots/papers-please-1.jpg` and `papers-please-2.jpg`:** the same paperwork style. Form A-20, the stamps and the memo match it.
- **`diegetic/shots/orwell-1.jpg`:** a dossier with a portrait and highlighted facts, like C's WATCH memo.
- **`diegetic/shots/shenzhen-io-2.jpg`:** printed paper as the interface. The Schedule form has that authority.
- **`reference-games/shots/suzerain-1.jpg`:** documents on a desk driving a turn.

**Verdict:** average (upper half). **Belongs here:** yes.

What holds it back:
1. The Action Cards menu is a big grid of near-identical manila tiles, the least crafted screen in C.
2. The placeholder operator is now the largest flat black shape on the home screen.

**Against Round 1:** better. Round 1's home was a crowded card file plus form. Now the office fills the screen and the desk objects open menus.

## E. Rooms and Stations
References:
- **`diegetic/shots/frostpunk-1.jpg`:** a full-screen world with a few instruments over it. E's home (about 80% art, a hanging P(Doom) dial) is the closest of ours to this.
- **`diegetic/shots/xcom-2-1.jpg`:** a base you look into, with the threat readout always on top.
- **`diegetic/shots/game-dev-tycoon-2.jpg`:** an office you watch grow, with a few overlays.
- **`diegetic/shots/hypnospace-outlaw-1.jpg`:** a fixed frame with places that change underneath (the five station buttons).

**Verdict:** better. It is the most cinematic of ours and reads cleanly. **Belongs here:** yes.

What holds it back:
1. The five station buttons are plain dark rectangles that do not look like objects in the room.
2. The Actions menu is a plain two-column list.

**Against Round 1:** clearly better. Round 1 was the busiest of the three; Round 2 has the emptiest, calmest home.

## G. The Existing UI, Modernised
References:
- **`reference-games/shots/football-manager-2024-1.jpg`:** a dense management UI with flat panels.
- **`reference-games/shots/democracy-4-1.jpg`:** a clean flat UI for a complex policy game.
- **`diegetic/shots/frostpunk-1.jpg`:** world art with a gauge and a side panel, like G's doom ring and plan card over the office.
- **`diegetic/shots/hacknet-1.jpg`:** a dark labelled-pane interface.

**Verdict:** average. It is clean, legible and consistent, but generic. **Belongs here:** yes.

What holds it back:
1. Flat purple and steel app panels sit on top of the art, not in it, so it has the least "office with computers" feel of the four.
2. The right column (doom card, Attention, plan) is still the densest home of ours.

**Against today's game (`G/before-m5.png`):** far better. Today's screen fails this test: overlapping text, a modal, near-black panels and debug labels. G clears the bar.

## Below the bar
None, so no lifting changes are needed. The "holds back" points above are non-blocking.
