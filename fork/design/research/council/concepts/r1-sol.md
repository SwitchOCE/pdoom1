## Ranking

1. **E. Rooms and Stations** gives the player distinct places to work, keeps the dated plan readable beside a door, and makes staff tangible on the Lab Floor. Its fixed instruments and Commit make room changes easy to follow.
2. **A. The Desk** makes the physical lab most convincing. The brass doom dial, actual folder fan, and swivel into WATCH give the two phases different jobs and moods. The rail and queue still need more room for play.
3. **B. The Mainframe OS** has the cleanest action scan and most legible November timeline. Mail makes a natural WATCH interruption. It ties A on score, with A ahead for Alex's office atmosphere and the stronger WATCH composition.
4. **C. The Paperwork Desk** makes a memorable workflow out of filling, stamping, and filing the same form. Its oversized card file competes with the editable schedule, and the form needs more room for controls and longer entries.
5. **D. The Card Table** makes Pip's hand metaphor literal and carries it into WATCH. Its illustrated top-level hand is clearer than its overflow queue, where four plays lose individual editing.
6. **F. Pure Terminal** is coherent retro software with good doom and reputation readouts. Its nearly uniform phosphor treatment flattens the hierarchy, and its most useful interaction covers the plan.
