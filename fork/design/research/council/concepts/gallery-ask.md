## What to do

Judge as a player would see a store screenshot: flavour, clarity, polish.
For each shortlisted concept (A, C, E):

1. Pick the 3-5 references from the gallery that are the right comparison
   for it (same register or job), and say why each.
2. Answer: among those references, is it **one of the better ones**,
   **about average**, or **below**? Does it "feel like it belongs here"?
   Give the one or two things that most hold it back from the next level,
   if any.

Then name which concept(s), if any, meet Alex's bar ("belongs here" and at
least average).

Scope: this is a judgement, not a new fix round. If a concept is below the
bar, name the smallest concrete changes that would lift it (view +
location), and mark everything else non-blocking.

## Answer format

- First line: `GALLERY: <letters that meet the bar, or NONE>` (e.g.
  `GALLERY: A C`).
- Per concept: chosen references (file names) with one line each; verdict
  (better / average / below); belongs here (yes/no); what holds it back.
- If any concept is below: the smallest lifting changes.
- Disk report.
