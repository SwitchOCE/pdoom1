## A. The Desk

**References chosen (5):**
- `not-for-broadcast-1.jpg` -- Same first-person hardware-console desk register: separate physical gauges, chunky glowing buttons, and a centre monitor.
- `papers-please-1.jpg` -- Same tactile desk conceit where physical slips, folders, and red stamps carry the rules and actions.
- `frostpunk-1.jpg` -- Same single dominant threat dial (`-110C` vs brass `P(DOOM)`) anchoring a warm-vs-dread palette.
- `shenzhen-io-1.jpg` -- Same late-night engineering workstation mood (dark monitor + work queue over a moody office backdrop).
- `ftl-1.jpg` -- Same visible-unit budget pips spent directly on actions with a fixed, unmistakable Commit/JUMP control.

- **Verdict:** better (one of the better ones)
- **Belongs here:** yes
- **What holds it back:**
  1. **Placeholder silhouette and clean CSS surfaces (`A/watch.png`, `A/m5.png`):** The smooth geometric dome-and-chair operator in `watch.png` reads as placeholder art next to the painted office behind it, and the desk hardware lacks the lived-in wear/scuffs that give `not-for-broadcast-1.jpg` its physical grit.
  2. **Office nearly hidden in PLAN (`A/m1.png`, `A/m5.png`):** Hardware panels fill almost the entire PLAN frame, so the "office with a bunch of computers" feel only really lands when the chair swivels in `watch.png`.
