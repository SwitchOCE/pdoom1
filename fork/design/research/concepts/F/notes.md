# F. Pure Terminal -- notes

**Concept.** The whole screen is the lab's mainframe terminal: double-ruled labelled panes, amber/green phosphor, a fixed status line and an F-key bar; every action is a hotkey and a clickable row. Doors are directories (`Scouting/`, `DIR >`) that open out to the right (ABBBCCC), so the hand reads like `ls -l` and the plan like a job queue.

**Places.** PLAN (m1, m5): ACTIONS | ATTENTION + THE MONTH AHEAD | THREAT BOARD, UNDER WAY (dated gantt), CAM-01 office feed, STAFF. F3 opens PROCUREMENT / UPGRADES (place2), ESC returns; F1 Guide, F2 Staff, F4 Ledger, F5 research quality. F10/Enter commits. In WATCH the centre becomes CAM-00, the silhouetted founder at the desk; events arrive as an auto-paused INCOMING window with numbered choices.

**Borrows from.** Hacknet (labelled panes, state apart from log), Norton/Midnight Commander (F-keys, directories), Uplink (status line), Duskers (camera feeds), Papers Please (event slip, fixed choices), XCOM 2 (coarse doom cells beside the exact %).

**Does well.** Every action, cost and blocked reason visible at once, no hover; plan vs op hours as colour + word + column; one home per fact; all ASCII-renderable in Godot, no icons to mismatch; scanlines only under text and on feeds.

**Weakest.** Dense and text-heavy, the least "office" of the set: diegesis rests on the terminal fiction and two camera panes, not the room. A 17-row list is drier than cards.

**Quality pass (all four renders).** Siblings left-aligned, numbers in fixed columns; no icons; text 14px+ (secondary only), controls 16px+; fixed overflow in action COST/STATUS, plan footer, upgrade descriptions; event and flyout stacked above camera layers; warm grounds, no near-black; prefixed class names. Not fixed: WATCH feed half empty on day 12 (fills as the month runs); m5 queue hand-picked to total 12 planning hours.
