# Grim pass round 1 consensus -- opus

## On the blocking items

**1. (sol, neon) .dim covers the instruments, rack, Held and Commit in
states 2, 3 and 5.** Agree, and I withdraw my "no damage against A". I
measured only the rack (3.38) and took it for dimmed background. On
s5.png the meter is clearly veiled: "56.7%", SEVERE and "band 4 of 7"
sit at roughly half the brightness they have in s1. The orchestrator
confirms .dim is added after the meters, rack and Commit (concept.html:616,
762, 805). It is also the same rule all three of us set for C in the hifi
round: the P(Doom) counter stays above the dim. Hifi A keeps these
surfaces lit. Their fix is right: insert .dim directly after room() (in
state 5 also after the left terminal and the cat print). Then re-measure
contrast on the composited PNGs, not the CSS colours. Keep the lit
ACTIONS/PLAN plate at full strength.

**2. (sol) The dateline types in again within November.** Agree, as a
cheap blocking item. The brief (item 5) and H's own notes say it "types in
on a month change". States 2, 3 and 4 replay it, and WATCH re-types the
whole date for a single day step. The renders are static (?still=1), so
only the live mock-up is affected. But that mock-up is what shows the
behaviour to Pip. Fix as sol says:
- animate only on a month's entry (states 1, 5->December, 6);
- in-month states, and WATCH's day changes, show the date already set,
  or change the day digits without the strike;
- keep the reduced-motion rule.

**3. (sol) State 4: "PAUSED FOR YOU" beside "MONTH RUNNING".** Agree.
Two adjacent labels contradict each other, which is a display bug under
AGENTS.md section 1. The fix is one string (concept.html:586). Use "MONTH
IN PLAY" (as in G) rather than "AWAITING REPLY", because the slot is
inert all through WATCH, not only while an event is open.

  the keycaps on disabled plates (A, P, U). While Talent Opportunity
