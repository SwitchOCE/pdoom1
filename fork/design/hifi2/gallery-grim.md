# Reference gallery, grim only (hifi2)

Sorted by the orchestrator on 2026-11-22 for Alex's steer:

> "junk anything in the gallery that doesn't [feel] pretty grim, pretty grim
> dark in terms of its vibe ... Look at Frostpunk's menus."

Kept images stay in fork/design/research/reference-games/shots/ and
diegetic/shots/. Dropped images were moved (not deleted) to
fork/design/research/_dropped-refs/ (same subfolders) on the Optiplex; to
reverse, move them back. None of these images is committed (third-party).
fork/design/hifi2/gallery.html shows the kept set.
